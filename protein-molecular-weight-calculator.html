How it works

      
The method behind the numbers

    

    

      
How molecular weight is calculated
The tool adds the average mass of each free amino acid in your sequence, then subtracts one water molecule (18.015 Da) for every peptide bond, that is, length − 1 waters. The result is the average mass of the unmodified chain in daltons and kilodaltons.

      
How GRAVY is calculated
GRAVY, the grand average of hydropathy, is the mean of the Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy values of all residues. Positive values suggest a hydrophobic protein, such as a membrane protein; negative values a more hydrophilic, soluble one.

      
What it does not include
Post-translational modifications, disulfide bonds, signal peptides that are cleaved off, and tags are not counted, so the mass you see on a gel or by mass spectrometry can differ.

    

  





  

    

      
Good to know

      
How the weight is calculated

      
The tool adds the average isotopic masses of the amino acids and subtracts one water molecule (18.015 Da) for each peptide bond, the standard average-mass estimate. X counts as an average residue. Post-translational modifications and disulfide bonds are not included.
