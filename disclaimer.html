General information

The content of this website describes our company, services and trainings. It is general information, not medical, clinical, legal or financial advice.

In silico results

We take full responsibility for every analysis we perform, and we stand behind our results. Computational results such as docking scores, simulations and vaccine designs are predictions made in silico. They guide research; before any clinical, diagnostic or commercial use they need confirmation in the laboratory.

Publication

We help researchers publish their research, but acceptance decisions belong to journals, reviewers and examiners, and we cannot promise any particular outcome.

Figures and samples

Figures such as the number of projects completed and students trained come from our own records. The sample graphs and 3D scenes show the kind of output and science we work with; each 3D scene carries a caption that says what it shows and whether it is real data or a built model.
