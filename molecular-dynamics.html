Molecular dynamics

Sample simulation output

Every graph on this page comes from one BioInfoX run: a 300-residue vaccine construct simulated for 100 ns in GROMACS and analysed with our standard pipeline. A simulation project receives the same set of analyses, as images and xmgrace files, with a written report.

The run

System
300-residue multi-epitope vaccine construct in water
Software
GROMACS 2026.3; analysis with GROMACS tools and MDTraj
Force field
CHARMM36m with CHARMM-modified TIP3P water
Box
Dodecahedron, 1.2 nm from protein to wall, neutralised with 0.15 M NaCl
Equilibration
200 ps NVT, then 500 ps NPT with heavy-atom restraints
Production
100 ns NPT at 310 K and 1 bar, 2 fs time step, 10,000 frames saved
1.22 nmMean backbone RMSD
2.11 nmMean radius of gyration
0.868Native contacts kept at 100 ns
72.0%Motion in the first two principal components
