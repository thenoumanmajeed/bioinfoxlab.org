01 Docking and dynamics

We watch proteins move.

Docking finds where a molecule binds. Molecular dynamics shows whether it stays there. We run simulations from 100 ns to 2,000 ns, up to ten in parallel, and a 100 ns run takes about 3 days. You receive trajectories, graphs, a movie and a written report.

    Molecular docking

    Docking, virtual screening and interaction analysis

02 Quantum chemistry and materials

Electrons, orbitals and materials.

Density functional theory gives the electronic picture behind reactivity and stability: optimised geometries, HOMO–LUMO gaps and electrostatic potential maps, for drug molecules and for materials such as photocatalysts and perovskite solar cells. Results for one molecule in about 2 days.

  1. 03

    DFT and quantum chemistry

    Geometry optimization, HOMO–LUMO, MEP and electronic properties

04 Networks and prediction

Targets, networks and models.

Our responsibility

We take full responsibility for every analysis we perform.

01

World-class analysis

Every study is set up, run and checked by working researchers, to the standard a peer-reviewed journal expects.

02

Open to challenge, anywhere

Our results can be challenged by your supervisor, a reviewer or another laboratory. We show our methods, settings and data so that anyone can check them.

03

We answer for every result

If a result is questioned, we explain how it was produced. If we made an error, we correct it and rerun the work at no charge.
