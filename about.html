About

A pioneering bioinformatics company from Lahore

BioInfoX (SMC-Private) Limited has served researchers around the world since 2022 and has trained more than 2,000 students in Pakistan.

Who we are

BioInfoX is one of the pioneering companies in Pakistan working in bioinformatics and computational biology, and the first registered bioinformatics company based in Lahore. Since 2022 we have served researchers all over the world.

Pioneering

Built from the start around bioinformatics, simulation and computational chemistry, at a time when few companies in Pakistan offered this work.

Worldwide

Researchers anywhere can send us a project. We work remotely and deliver the data, the figures and a written report.

Training

More than 2,000 students trained and 200 interns mentored through our online programs in Pakistan.

About the founder

The researcher who built BioInfoX

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Muhammad Nouman Majeed

PhD Scholar · Bioinformatician

Muhammad Nouman Majeed founded BioInfoX and leads it today. His research covers in silico vaccine design, molecular dynamics simulations and computational drug discovery, with a focus on protein–ligand interactions and multi-epitope vaccines. Alongside BioInfoX he works as a Research Associate at The University of Lahore.
