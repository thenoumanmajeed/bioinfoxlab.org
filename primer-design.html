How it works

The method behind the numbers

How candidates are chosen
The tool scans both strands of your template for primers that meet three rules: 40–60% GC, a basic Tm between 52 and 68 °C, and no run of four or more identical bases.
How pairs are ranked
Forward and reverse candidates are paired when their product falls in your size range. Each pair is scored by the difference between the two Tm values, whether each primer ends in a G or C (a GC clamp), and how close each one is to 50% GC. Lower scores rank higher.
What this basic designer does not check
It does not test for primer dimers, hairpins or binding elsewhere in a genome. Check the best pair with in-silico PCR and against your organism's genome before ordering.

Good to know

A basic designer, honestly labelled

Pairs here satisfy the standard first-pass rules (GC 40–60%, Tm 52–68 °C, matched Tm within a pair). Before ordering, run the winning pair through the in-silico PCR checker on this site and a dimer check — and remember salt and primer concentration shift real Tm values.
