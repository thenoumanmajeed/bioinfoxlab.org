Muhammad Zohaib

BS Bioinformatics (final year, expected July 2027), University of Agriculture Faisalabad (UAF)

Muhammad Zohaib is a final-year BS Bioinformatics student at the University of Agriculture Faisalabad, specializing in interpretable machine learning for transcriptomic biomarker discovery in oncology.
