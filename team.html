On-Demand Bioinformatics & Machine Learning Consultant

Muhammad Zohaib

Bioinformatics, University of Agriculture Faisalabad (UAF)

Muhammad Zohaib specializes in interpretable machine learning for transcriptomic biomarker discovery in oncology. His work applies leakage-free XGBoost and SHAP pipelines to omics data and extends into structure-based drug repurposing using AlphaFold, AutoDock Vina, PyRx and Discovery Studio. He has submitted a first-author manuscript to PLOS ONE, has a second in preparation, and builds reproducible, well-documented workflows for biological and research datasets.

  • Interpretable machine learning
  • Transcriptomic biomarkers
  • XGBoost and SHAP
  • Drug repurposing

On-Demand RNA-Seq & Transcriptomics Consultant

Diderul Islam

BSc in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (in progress), Dhaka International University

Through the RNA Institute’s Bioinformatics Research Program, Diderul Islam processed two independent glioblastoma RNA-seq datasets from raw sequencing data through differential gene expression analysis and functional interpretation, used the findings to prioritize potential therapeutic candidates, and documented the methods and software settings at every stage. Diderul also brings five years of experience in science communication, turning scientific findings into clear, accessible language.

Tahira Rashid holds a Master’s degree in Genetics from the University of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, Lahore, and a Bachelor’s degree in Biological Sciences. On-Demand Computational Chemistry & DFT Consultant

Vitalis Mbayo

PhD in Chemistry (Computational Chemistry & Molecular Modelling), University of Johannesburg · MSc (Cum Laude), University of Johannesburg

Vitalis Mbayo is a Computational Medicinal Chemist specializing in molecular modeling, Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations, and computer-aided drug discovery. His research centers on atomistic simulations, reaction profiling, and targeted therapeutic design. Combining academic research with prior industry experience in regulatory affairs and pharmacovigilance at the Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe, he brings a rigorous approach to evaluating molecular mechanisms and drug targets. He has also mentored undergraduate and postgraduate scientists in molecular modeling and computational workflows.

  • DFT calculations
  • Molecular modelling
  • Computer-aided drug discovery
  • Reaction profiling

On-Demand Network Pharmacology Consultant

Dr. Fariha Javaid

PhD in Biotechnology, University of the Punjab, Lahore, Pakistan

Dr. Fariha Javaid holds a PhD in Biotechnology from the University of the Punjab, Lahore, Pakistan. Her expertise includes computer-aided drug discovery (CADD), network pharmacology, AI-assisted drug discovery, machine learning, and immunoinformatics. She specializes in molecular docking, protein–protein interaction network analysis, functional enrichment analysis, and computational identification of potential therapeutic targets. She also uses R for scientific data analysis and visualization and Google Colab for computational research workflows. Her research interests focus on integrating bioinformatics and computational approaches to investigate disease mechanisms and support drug discovery.

  • Network pharmacology
