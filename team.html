Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Muhammad Nouman Majeed

PhD Scholar · Bioinformatician

Muhammad Nouman Majeed leads BioInfoX. His research covers in silico vaccine design, molecular dynamics and computational drug discovery, with 32 peer-reviewed articles to date. He is a Gold Medalist in M.Sc. Biotechnology from the University of Okara and a Research Associate at The University of Lahore.
