About

PhD scholar and bioinformatician working from epitope to trajectory: designing vaccine and drug candidates in silico and testing them with molecular dynamics.

I specialise in in silico vaccine design, molecular dynamics simulations and computational drug discovery. My research focuses on protein–ligand interactions, molecular modelling and multi-epitope vaccine development, and I have contributed to research projects and publications in molecular dynamics, immunoinformatics and AI-assisted drug discovery.

I founded and lead BioInfoX (SMC-Private) Limited, which provides molecular docking, molecular dynamics, DFT and bioinformatics services to researchers worldwide and trains students across Pakistan.

In silico vaccine design

Epitope prediction and multi-epitope vaccine constructs against viral, bacterial and fungal pathogens.

Molecular dynamics

Protein–ligand and protein–membrane simulations with GROMACS, OpenMM and ACEMD3, and binding free energies.

Computational drug discovery

Docking, virtual screening, QSAR and AI-driven screening and optimisation of ligands.

Nanoinformatics and DFT

DFT, neural networks and simulation to study nanoparticles for therapy and environmental use.

Genomics

Whole-genome sequencing, assembly and NCBI submission of bacterial isolates.

AI in bioinformatics

Artificial neural networks for protein structure, genomics and biomarker discovery.
