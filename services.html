Services

Eight service lines, one research partner

Send us your structures, sequences or research question. We set up the study, run it, analyse the results and help you publish them: more than 1,000 projects completed for researchers worldwide.

What we do

Eight lines of work, each with its own method

  1. 01

    Molecular docking

    Docking, virtual screening and interaction analysis. Typical turnaround: 2 days

    Molecular docking: details
  2. 02

    Molecular dynamics

    Simulation setup, trajectory analysis and binding-energy estimation You receive trajectories and topologies, graphs as images and xmgrace files, structure snapshots, a trajectory movie and a written report. Typical turnaround: 3 days

    Molecular dynamics: details
  3. 03

    DFT and quantum chemistry

    Geometry optimization, HOMO–LUMO, MEP and electronic properties. Typical turnaround: 2 days

    DFT and quantum chemistry: details
  4. 04

    Bioinformatics

    Sequence analysis, protein modelling and biological data analysis. Typical turnaround: 3–5 days

    Bioinformatics: details
  5. 05

    Network pharmacology

    Target networks, enrichment analysis and Cytoscape visualizations. Typical turnaround: 3 days

    Network pharmacology: details
  6. 06

    Immunoinformatics

    Epitope prediction and in silico vaccine design. Typical turnaround: 5 days

    Immunoinformatics: details
  7. 07

    AI and cheminformatics

    QSAR, molecular descriptors and predictive models. Typical turnaround: 5–7 days

    AI and cheminformatics: details
  8. 08

    Training and consulting

    Workshops, research guidance and customized training. Typical turnaround: On schedule

    Training and consulting: details

Turnaround

How long it takes

Typical times for one standard system, counted from the day we receive your files. Larger studies get their own timeline with the quote.

  1. 01

    Molecular docking

    2 days

    Docking, virtual screening and interaction analysis of one target

  2. 02

    Molecular dynamics

    3 days

    Per 100 ns simulation. Runs from 100 ns to 2,000 ns, up to ten in parallel

  3. 03

    DFT and quantum chemistry

    2 days

    Geometry optimization, HOMO–LUMO and MEP for one molecule

  4. 04

    Bioinformatics

    3–5 days

    Depending on the size of the data set

  5. 05

    Network pharmacology

    3 days

    Targets, enrichment analysis and Cytoscape figures

  6. 06

    Immunoinformatics

    5 days

    Epitope prediction through to a vaccine construct

  7. 07

    AI and cheminformatics

    5–7 days

    Descriptors, QSAR and a validated predictive model

  8. 08

    Training and consulting

    On schedule

    Workshops and guidance arranged around your dates

  1. STEP 1

    Send your brief

    Your structures or sequences, the question you want answered and your deadline.

  2. STEP 2

    Agree the scope

    We confirm the method and deliverables, then send a quote and a timeline.

  3. STEP 3

    We run the study

    Up to ten simulations run in parallel, so results arrive sooner.

  4. STEP 4

    Receive your results

    Data, figures and a written report, with answers to your questions.

Trajectories and topologies, graphs as images and xmgrace files, structure snapshots at chosen time points, a trajectory movie and a written report.
