Services

Eight service lines, one research partner

Send us your structures, sequences or research question. We set up the study, run it, analyse the results and write them up in a form your paper or thesis can use.

  1. 01

    Molecular docking

    Docking, virtual screening and interaction analysis

  2. 02

    Molecular dynamics

    Simulation setup, trajectory analysis and binding-energy estimation. See sample output

  3. 03

    DFT and quantum chemistry

    Geometry optimization, HOMO–LUMO, MEP and electronic properties

  4. 04

    Bioinformatics

    Sequence analysis, protein modelling and biological data analysis

  5. 05

    Network pharmacology

    Target networks, enrichment analysis and Cytoscape visualizations

  6. 06

    Immunoinformatics

    Epitope prediction and in silico vaccine design

  7. 07

    AI and cheminformatics

    QSAR, molecular descriptors and predictive models

  8. 08

    Training and consulting

    Workshops, research guidance and customized training

  1. STEP 1

    Send your brief

    Your structures or sequences, the question you want answered and your deadline.

  2. STEP 2

    Agree the scope

    We confirm the method and deliverables, then send a quote and a timeline.

  3. STEP 3

    We run the study

    Up to ten simulations run in parallel, so results arrive sooner.

  4. STEP 4

    Receive your results

    Data, figures and a written report, with answers to your questions.

Simulation projects include

Trajectories and topologies, graphs as images and xmgrace files, structure snapshots at chosen time points, a trajectory movie and a written report.
