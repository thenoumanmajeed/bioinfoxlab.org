Who we are

This website is run by BioInfoX (SMC-Private) Limited, a company registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (CUIN 0352412) and based in Lahore, Pakistan. For anything in this policy, write to info@bioinfoxlab.org.

What this website collects

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it has no user accounts. It sets no cookies (see the Cookie Policy).

The project brief form on the contact page does not send anything to us or store anything. It prepares a message in your own email app, and nothing reaches us unless you choose to send it.

Information you send us

When you email us, message us on WhatsApp, send project files or register for a training, we receive what you choose to share, for example:

  • your name, email address, phone number and institution;
  • your project files: structures, sequences, data sets and research questions;
  • training registration details and payment confirmations.

We use this information to reply to you, prepare a quote, carry out your project, run trainings and issue certificates. We do not use it for anything else.

Your research data

Your files, results and research questions are confidential. We use them only for your project. We do not share them, publish them, or use them in our own work or marketing without your written permission. The data and results we deliver belong to you.

Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only when we must to deliver your project or training, for example through our email provider or a file-transfer service, or when Pakistani law requires it.

Third-party services on this website

  • Google Fonts. Your browser loads the Montserrat typeface from Google, which receives your IP address to deliver it. Google’s own privacy policy applies.
  • WhatsApp. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp, run by Meta. Messages you send there are covered by WhatsApp’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, page requested and time) for security and reliability.
  • External links. Links to LinkedIn, Google Scholar, NCBI, the SECP and other sites are covered by those sites’ own policies.

How long we keep information

We keep project files for as long as the project and any follow-up questions need them, and we delete them when you ask us to. We keep invoices and payment records for as long as Pakistani law requires.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us, including limiting who on our team can see project files.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@bioinfoxlab.org and we will reply.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
