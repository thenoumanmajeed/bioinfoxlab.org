Service 06

Immunoinformatics and in silico vaccine design

Multi-epitope vaccine design from proteome to construct: epitope prediction, antigenicity, allergenicity and toxicity screening, construct design, receptor docking and molecular dynamics. About 5 days.

Request a quote Ask on WhatsApp

What we do

The work, step by step

  1. 01

    Antigen selection

    Choosing target proteins from the pathogen’s proteome.

  2. 02

    Epitope prediction

    B-cell and T-cell (MHC class I and II) epitopes, screened for antigenicity, allergenicity and toxicity.

  3. 03

    Construct design

    Epitopes joined with linkers and an adjuvant into a multi-epitope vaccine construct, with its physicochemical properties.

  4. 04

    Structure and validation

    Three-dimensional model of the construct, refined and validated.

  5. 05

    Receptor docking and dynamics

    Docking to immune receptors and molecular dynamics to test the stability of the complex.

You receive

  • Epitope tables
  • The vaccine construct sequence and model
  • Docking and dynamics results
  • A written methods and results section

Typical turnaround

5 days from proteome to construct

Counted from the day we receive your files. Larger studies get their own timeline with the quote. All turnaround times

Tools

Standard epitope-prediction and screening servers, structure prediction and refinement, docking, and GROMACS for molecular dynamics. The founder has published many multi-epitope vaccine designs (see Research).

Who works on it

Muhammad Nouman Majeed, with the BioInfoX research team. We take full responsibility for every analysis we perform.
