Good to know

      
Which Tm should you trust?

      
The Wallace rule (2 °C per A/T, 4 °C per G/C) suits short primers under ~14 nt. For longer primers the salt-adjusted estimate is the better guide, and nearest-neighbour calculators (like your thermocycler software) refine it further with salt and primer concentrations. Design primer pairs within ~2 °C of each other.
